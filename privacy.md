# Privacy Policy — Ctrl+A

_Last updated: 2026-07-24_

Ctrl+A does not collect, store, or transmit any user data.

- The extension runs only on the tab you are viewing, and only when you click its
  button. It reads that page's content solely to convert it to Markdown, entirely
  within your browser.
- No data is sent to any server. There is no backend, no analytics, and no
  tracking of any kind.
- The Markdown you copy or download stays on your device.
- Permissions used: `activeTab` and `scripting`, both required to read the current
  page's content on demand. No `history`, no `tabs` background access, no host
  permissions to third-party sites.

If you have questions, contact: contact@lifehack-with.com
